### Added

- `OverlayStartupLocation.TopRight`: an overlay window opens at its parent window's top-right corner, its `Left` and
  `Top` the room from the right and top edges; once open, they are its place as for any other.

### Changed

- Sandbox: the Diagnostics switch shows the live diagnostics in an overlay window at the top-right - moved where they
  do not cover the page, collapsed to its title when not read - instead of a plate fixed in the window's corner; its
  close button turns the switch off. The ribbon window has the same switch, its presentation choice in the window.
