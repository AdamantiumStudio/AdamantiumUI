### Added

- macOS: the Mac's accessibility reads lists, tabs, tables and outlines: what is selected, and selects; an item's place
  among its own; a tab group's tabs, the open one its value, a tab's or radio button's being selected; a table's rows,
  columns, column headers and the cell at a row and column, a cell's row and column; an outline's rows shown, a row's
  depth and place among them, whether it is open - and opening and shutting it - and the rows under and over it.

### Changed

- macOS: the accessibility bridge tells nothing of what changes once nothing was asked for a minute and VoiceOver,
  Zoom and Switch Control are off; a question, or one of them turned on, has it told again.

### Fixed

- macOS: a table's editable cell is a cell to the Mac's accessibility, not a row.
