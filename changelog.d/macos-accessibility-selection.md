### Added

- macOS: the Mac's accessibility reads lists, tabs, tables and outlines: what is selected, and selects; an item's place
  among its own; a tab group's tabs, the open one its value, a tab's or radio button's being selected; a table's rows,
  columns, column headers and the cell at a row and column, a cell's row and column; an outline's rows shown, a row's
  depth, whether it is open - and opening and shutting it - and the rows under and over it.

### Changed

- macOS: the accessibility bridge stops listening to what changes once nothing was asked for a minute and VoiceOver
  is off, and starts again at the next question.
